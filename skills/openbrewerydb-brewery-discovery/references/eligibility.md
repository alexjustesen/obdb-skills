# Eligibility

Use the live Open Brewery DB documentation and contribution guidance as the final authority because dataset policy can change. This reference supplies discovery triage, not a replacement schema.

## Usually eligible for investigation

- A brewery or brewpub producing beer at an identified location.
- A public taproom or additional brewery location when it independently fits current dataset policy.
- A cidery or other establishment explicitly covered by current Open Brewery DB policy.
- Planning or closed operations when the live dataset supports those statuses and reliable evidence establishes the facts.

## Requires verification

- Contract, alternating-proprietorship, or brand-only operations.
- Production facilities with no public-facing operation.
- Brewery-owned bars, restaurants, bottle shops, and taprooms that may not brew on site.
- Guild members whose membership class includes suppliers, distributors, enthusiasts, or allied businesses.
- Regulator records representing legal entities, warehouses, mailing addresses, duplicate licenses, or expired premises.
- Businesses described only by social media, maps, or an old directory.
- Temporarily closed, seasonal, mobile, virtual, or not-yet-open operations.

## Exclude or mark out of scope

- Entries outside the declared geographic boundary.
- Suppliers, distributors, wholesalers, equipment vendors, event companies, and membership organizations.
- Homebrewers and private/noncommercial clubs unless current policy explicitly includes them.
- Duplicate administrative/license entries for one physical operation.
- Leads with no reliable evidence of an eligible operation or location.
- Records whose source cannot be lawfully reused, when no independent permitted evidence can verify the candidate.

## Decision record

For each non-obvious candidate, record:

- The relevant current dataset policy.
- What is produced and where.
- Whether the location is public-facing and independently addressable.
- Operating/planning/closed status and evidence date.
- Relationship to parent brands and sibling locations.
- Inclusion or exclusion rationale and confidence.

Do not turn uncertainty into a missing-brewery claim. Put unresolved eligibility in `Status verification`; put confirmed ineligible or out-of-scope entries in `Exclusions`.
